## 1. Objective
Build and evaluate a multiple linear regression model to predict residential property prices in Bengaluru based on square footage, bedroom count (BHK), bathrooms, and property age.

## 2. Evaluation Metrics
* **R-squared ($R^2$):** Measures the proportion of variance in house prices explained by the features. 
* **RMSE:** Quantifies the average magnitude of prediction errors in Lakhs (₹).

## 3. Coefficient Analysis
* **Area (SqFt):** Positive coefficient indicates price scales directly with apartment size.
* **Property Age:** Negative coefficient demonstrates depreciation over time.
