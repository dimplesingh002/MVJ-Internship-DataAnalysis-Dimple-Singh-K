\# Internship Project: Business Questions



\* \*\*Dataset Name:\*\* Zomato Bengaluru Restaurants

\* \*\*Source:\*\* Kaggle Datasets (\[Zomato Bangalore Restaurants](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/himanshupoddar/zomato-bangalore-restaurants) / Kaggle Search)\[cite: 8]

\* \*\*Track:\*\* Data Analysis



\## Business Questions



1\. \*\*Online Ordering vs. Ratings:\*\* Do restaurants that offer online ordering achieve higher average aggregate ratings compared to those that do not?

2\. \*\*Cost vs. Cuisines:\*\* What are the top 5 most expensive cuisines in Bengaluru on average based on the "cost for two" metric?

3\. \*\*Popularity vs. Quality:\*\* Is there a strong statistical correlation between the total number of votes a restaurant receives and its aggregate rating?

4\. \*\*Geographic Hotspots:\*\* Which specific neighborhoods (locations) in Bengaluru have the highest concentration of premium-rated restaurants (aggregate rating above 4.0)?

5\. \*\*Table Booking Impact:\*\* How does the availability of table booking affect the average cost for two across different restaurant types?
