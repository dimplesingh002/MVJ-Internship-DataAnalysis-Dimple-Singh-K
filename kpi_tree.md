# KPI Tree: Namma Metro (Bengaluru)

## 🎯 Top-Level Objective
Maximize operational efficiency, passenger satisfaction, and financial sustainability for Namma Metro.

---

## 🌳 KPI Breakdown Structure

### 1. Total Ridership (Volume & Growth)
* **Daily Average Riders:** Total tap-ins per day across all stations.
* **Monthly Active Commuters:** Unique smart card / Metro app users per month.
* **Line-wise Split:** Purple Line vs. Green Line passenger distribution.

### 2. Peak Share (Capacity & Congestion)
* **Peak Hour Share (%):** Percentage of total daily riders traveling during peak hours (8:00 AM–11:00 AM and 5:00 PM–8:00 PM).
* **Station Congestion Index:** Peak crowd density at major interchange stations (e.g., Majestic, Indiranagar, MG Road).
* **Train Occupancy Rate:** Average passenger count per coach vs. maximum capacity.

### 3. Revenue Proxies (Financials)
* **Farebox Revenue:** Total ticket sales (smart cards, paper tokens, WhatsApp/UPI QR tickets).
* **Non-Farebox Revenue:** Station naming rights, retail kiosk rentals inside stations, and train exterior advertising.
* **Average Revenue Per User (ARPU):** Total revenue divided by total unique riders.

---

## 📈 Connecting to the 4 Types of Analytics
* **Descriptive:** Tracking total monthly farebox revenue and daily ridership numbers.
* **Diagnostic:** Analyzing *why* certain stations have low non-farebox revenue or high bottlenecks during peak hours.
* **Predictive:** Forecasting next week's peak-hour passenger surges based on weather and weekday patterns.
* **Prescriptive:** Recommending specific operational adjustments (e.g., frequency increases or dynamic pricing/promotions for off-peak hours).
